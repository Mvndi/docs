# War Justification and Treaties

## War System – Staff Responsibilities

The war system requires active Admin oversight. No war may begin without Admin approval. Staff affiliated with any participating nation must not oversee that conflict.

## War Declaration Approval

- All war declarations must be submitted to an Admin or war manager via a ticket before any hostilities begin.

- The reviewing Admin must confirm: a valid Casus Belli applies,  required prior diplomatic attempts are documented (for War of Annexation), and neither they nor any available staff have a conflict of interest.

- If all Admins are affiliated with a participating nation, the war declaration is suspended until a non-affiliated Admin is available to review it.

- Approvals must be logged with: nations involved, Casus Belli type, approving Admin, and date.

- If you feel an admin is biased you can appeal it. If this is continuously used the war will be annulled and the side that appealed cannot declare war anymore.

- Diplomacy is when you deliberately try to ask for provinces or give an ultimatum. Diplomacy has to be roleplayed and not just “I want this. Now give it to me”. RP is crucial if you want your diplomacy to be valid for war declaration.

## Truces

- Truces are enforced by admins no matter what.

- Truce time will be determined by war admin that approved the war

- Truces can be appealed if too long or too short. Truce will then be looked over by high ranking admin

- Truces do not prevent raids nor other aggressions.

- Only war goal ignoring truces is treaty violation wars.

- minimum truce time is always one siege session

## Declaration of a coalition war

In order to declare a coalition war, there are three mandatory points that need to be respected and met by the accusing side.

- The coalition must have atleast TWO of the following reasons in order to have their coalition war granted:

| Reason | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Conquering strenght | The accused party has won THREE or more attacking sieges and conquered THREE towns in a frame of FIVE siege sessions, no matter if they belong to the same nation or different ones, MUST BE PROVEN WITH SCREENSHOTS |
| Menacing war strenght | The accused party has an incredible army's strenght, this is proven by the amount of men that show up when wars occurs, MUST BE PROVEN WITH SCREENSHOTS |
| Betrayal or treachery | The accused party has recently betrayed or disrespected a pact, treaty or agreement with one or more nations that are involved in the coalition, MUST BE PROVEN WITH SCREENSHOTS |
| Humiliation and insults | The accused party has recently humiliated and or insulted publicly atleast TWO of the nations involved in the coalition, MUST BE PROVEN WITH SCREENSHOTS |

- When the coalition war has their reasons approved, the accusing party MUST have a roleplay meeting that gives "birth" to the temporary coalition, all the coalition members must be present during that meeting. After the meeting occurs and gets clipped, the clip must be SENT to the STAFF in the war ticket to analyze the CLIP and give an approval or a denial to the coalition. If the meeting results non appropriate, it needs to get denied and therefore another meeting needs to take place, after the THIRD denial, the coalition war can't be approved anymore.

- Coalitions can stand for a maximum of 4 siege sessions and has a cooldown of 1 siege session. (This gives the chance to diplomacies to evolve themselves between the accused party and the accusing ones, when the cooldown is active, NO OTHER coalition war can be called upon the previously accused party by anyone).
