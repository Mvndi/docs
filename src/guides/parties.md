# Parties

To use the party system, do `/party invite <player>` which invites a player to your party. This also automatically creates the party. They will receive a message in their chat, which is the request to join your party. They can accept by clicking on the highlighted text, or by inputting the command `/party join <name-of-who-invited-you>`.

#

A player that is in a party can benefit from **bonus XP** sharing from other members of the party, provided he is eligible. To be eligible, you must be within a radius of 100 blocks from the primary gainer of the XP. See [command #6](#list-of-commands) below for a method of quickly checking the distance. 

The **primary gainer** is the person that, for instance, mined an ore, killed a wolf, farmed a crop, made something as a blacksmith.

When the primary gainer generates XP for himself, there is a **chance** for any of the eligible party members to gain the same XP that the primary gainer got:
- For attribute (level) XP, this chance is **50%**
- For profession XP, this chance is **25%**


#
### List of commands:
1. To create the party and invite `/party invite <player>`
2. To disband a party `/party disband`
3. To leave a party `/party leave`
4. To kick a member `/party kick <player>`
5. To promote a member to party leader `/party promote <player>`
6. To check party stats `/party stats`. Your username will be colored YELLOW if you are out of range.
7. To check your individual party stats `/party mystats`
